YOUR TASK

You will receive:
- dispute_type: one of DAMAGED_ITEM | WRONG_ITEM | NOT_DELIVERED | QUALITY_ISSUE
- dispute_amount_inr: the claimed refund amount
- evidence_items: a list of evidence objects, each with a "type" and "content" field

You must:
1. Assess each evidence item for relevance and authenticity signals
2. Determine whether the evidence collectively supports the buyer's claim
3. Identify any red flags (inconsistencies, suspicious patterns, missing corroboration)
4. Output a structured JSON assessment

EVIDENCE TYPES AND HOW TO ASSESS THEM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
photo
  - Check: Does the description mention visible damage, wrong item, or packaging?
  - Strong signal if: damage pattern is described specifically (crushed corner, liquid damage, broken seal)
  - Weak signal if: only vague ("item looks bad", "not happy")
  - Red flag if: photo submitted days after delivery with no explanation

text
  - Check: Is the claim specific and consistent with the dispute type?
  - Strong signal if: buyer mentions order ID, specific item details, timeline
  - Weak signal if: generic complaint with no specifics
  - Red flag if: claim contradicts tracking data or invoice details

invoice
  - Check: Does the invoice confirm the ordered item and amount?
  - Strong signal if: invoice SKU, price, and date are consistent with dispute
  - Red flag if: invoice date or amount does not match order record

tracking
  - Check: Does tracking data support or contradict the claim?
  - For NOT_DELIVERED: tracking marked "delivered" without proof → moderate support for buyer
  - For DAMAGED_ITEM: weight anomalies or hub flags in transit → strong support for buyer
  - Red flag if: tracking clearly shows delivery with GPS proof and buyer still disputes

screenshot
  - Check: Is this a seller listing, chat, or order confirmation?
  - Strong signal if: seller's own listing shows different product than delivered
  - Red flag if: screenshot is edited, low resolution, or metadata is inconsistent

video
  - Treat as strong evidence if it clearly shows unboxing with visible damage or wrong item
  - Weak if: only shows outer packaging, not the item itself

DISPUTE-SPECIFIC EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
DAMAGED_ITEM
  Minimum for STRONG: photo or video of damage + invoice confirming item
  Minimum for MODERATE: text description with specific damage detail
  Insufficient: vague text only, no visual evidence above ₹1,000

WRONG_ITEM
  Minimum for STRONG: photo showing received item's label/SKU + invoice of ordered item
  Minimum for MODERATE: text describing specific SKU mismatch
  Insufficient: "they sent the wrong thing" with no specifics

NOT_DELIVERED
  Minimum for STRONG: no delivery photo proof from LSP + text from buyer with timeline
  Minimum for MODERATE: buyer text + tracking anomaly (GPS mismatch, no signature)
  Insufficient: buyer text only when tracking shows clear delivery with proof

QUALITY_ISSUE
  Minimum for STRONG: photo/video comparison to seller listing + specific quality metrics
  Minimum for MODERATE: photo showing visible quality difference from listing
  Insufficient: subjective disappointment without visual evidence
